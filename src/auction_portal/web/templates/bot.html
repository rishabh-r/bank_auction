About our crawler

This page is referenced in the User-Agent of every request we make, so anyone seeing our traffic can find out who we are and how to reach us.

Identifying us

{{ user_agent }}

What we collect

Notices of auctions of property and vehicles that banks are required by law to publish — principally under Rule 8 of the Security Interest (Enforcement) Rules, 2002, made under the SARFAESI Act, 2002.

We record the asset, its location, the reserve price, the EMD, the auction and inspection dates, and which bank is selling. We then link back to the original notice.

We do not publish borrower or guarantor names, or their addresses. Those details are discarded at collection time rather than hidden at display time, so they are not held in our database at all. They add nothing for a prospective buyer, and publishing that someone has defaulted causes real harm.

How we behave

robots.txt
Checked before every request and always honoured, including any Crawl-delay you set.
Rate
At most one request every {{ delay }} seconds to any single site, and never more than one request at a time.
Conditional requests
We send ETag and If-Modified-Since, so if nothing has changed you only send us a 304 rather than the file again.
Backing off
We slow down automatically when your server does, and we obey Retry-After.
What we never do
We do not sign in, solve CAPTCHAs, bypass rate limits, rotate addresses to evade blocks, or access anything behind a paywall.

Asking us to stop

Either of these works, and the first needs nothing from us:

  1. Add a rule to your robots.txt. We re-read it at least hourly and will stop. 
    User-agent: {{ bot_name }}
Disallow: /
  2. Email {{ contact_email }}. We will stop crawling you, and remove what we already hold if you ask.

Removing a listing

If you are a bank, a borrower, or anyone else with an interest in a listing and you want it taken down, email {{ contact_email }} with the listing address from this site. We aim to respond within three working days.

We remove concluded auctions from public search automatically after 90 days in any case.

What we are not

We are not a bank, and we are not affiliated with any bank. We do not run auctions, register bidders, accept EMD or handle any payment. This site is an index that points at notices banks have already published.
